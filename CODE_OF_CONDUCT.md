# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Focus criticism on ideas and code, assume good intent, respect privacy, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or unwanted personal attention.

## Scope

These standards apply in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's security-reporting channel when appropriate. The maintainer may edit or remove content, reject contributions, or restrict participation to protect the project and its community.

Project decisions should be explained clearly and enforced consistently.
